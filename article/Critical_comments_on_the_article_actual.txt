2 Pareto frontier интерпретирован некорректно
В разделе 4.5 написано:
The Pareto frontier is dominated by JPEG variants from q88 through q25. No quantized BPC point lies on the frontier.
Это не соответствует представленным числам.
Напишите
Under a strict numerical definition, q94 and b7 also lie on the Pareto frontier. However, the b7 gain over the baseline is only 0.0002 mAP50 and should not be interpreted as a meaningful improvement. With a practical tolerance for negligible metric fluctuations, the useful frontier is represented by the JPEG variants.

В разделе 5.5 правильно сказано, что packed representation может быть эффективнее.

The BMP+LZMA experiment does not implement physical b-bit packing; it compresses byte-expanded 24-bit RGB representations. Packed low-bit streams remain a subject for future work.

8  В разделе 5.3 написано:
CNNs are highly robust to the high-frequency blocky boundaries and minor color shifts introduced by JPEG recompression, but they are severely disrupted by the loss of amplitude precision...
Эксперимент показывает различие результатов, но не исследует внутренние activations модели и не доказывает механизм.
Лучше написать
The observed results suggest that YOLOv8n is more tolerant of the tested JPEG recompression artifacts than of severe RGB level quantization. A feature-level analysis would be required to establish the underlying mechanism.

PSNR/SSIM is fundamentally flawed
лучше заменить более точным:
PSNR and SSIM are insufficient as sole predictors of downstream detection quality across different distortion families.
